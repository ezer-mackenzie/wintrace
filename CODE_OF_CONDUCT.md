# Code of Conduct

We want participation in the `wintrace` project to be respectful, constructive,
and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Give actionable, technically focused feedback.
- Accept that people may disagree; discuss ideas without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and disruptive
behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through the
[project maintainer profile](https://github.com/ezer-mackenzie). Do not post
sensitive details in a public issue.
